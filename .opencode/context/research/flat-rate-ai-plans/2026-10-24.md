## ModelsLab Correction

The 2026-10-24 check of `https://modelslab.com/pricing` replaced the former single
ModelsLab row with three:

| Plan | Price | Published allowance | Concurrency |
| :--- | ---: | :--- | ---: |
| Basic | $21/mo | 3,250 API calls/mo | 5 |
| Standard | $47/mo | 10,000 API calls/mo | 10 |
| Open Source Unlimited | $149/mo | Unlimited eligible ModelsLab-hosted open-source models | 15 |

Premium third-party models remain wallet-billed on every plan, including Open
Source Unlimited. The $149 label does not make the entire multi-modal catalogue
or every chat/LLM model unlimited. ModelsLab has no free tier, and an active
subscription is required before wallet funding is available.

## Current Provider Checks

The latest research pass verified and added official pricing details for:

- GitLab Duo Agent Platform: Free, Premium at $29/user/mo billed annually,
  Ultimate by custom quote; promotional monthly credits of 12 and 24 for
  Premium and Ultimate respectively, with additional GitLab Credits at $1 each.
- GitHub Copilot: Pro $10/mo, Pro+ $39/mo, Max $100/mo; monthly AI credits and
  optional paid overage.
- Kiro: Pro $20/mo, Pro+ $40/mo, Pro Max $100/mo, Power $200/mo; 1,000–10,000
  monthly credits and $0.04 add-on credits.
- Cognition Devin: Pro $20/mo, Max $200/mo, and Teams at $80/mo plus $40/mo per
  full developer seat.
- Cursor: a usage-pooled product with an $8–$200/mo individual range; third-party
  model usage is separate. The current site also carries Grok/X branding.
- Verdent: a credit-based $5–$179/mo range, with Eco Mode and paid top-ups.
- Augment Code: Standard $20/mo and Business $100/mo, each with pooled usage for
  up to 50 seats and pay-as-you-go top-ups.
- Sourcegraph: an enterprise contract starting at $16,000 per year, not a
  consumer flat-rate plan.

All seven plan-family rows above are present in the current canonical table.
Sourcegraph remains excluded because its entry point is an enterprise contract
rather than an individual flat-rate subscription.

## Research Rules

- Use first-party pricing and billing documentation first.
- Record the exact reset window, metering unit, and concurrency ceiling.
- Treat “unlimited” as scoped to named models or modalities unless the provider
  explicitly says the whole catalogue is exempt from metering.
- Keep historical captures separate from current conclusions.
- Re-fetch before publishing changed pricing, because plan names, quotas, and
  product ownership change quickly.
