# Worldbuilding — Locations, Systems, Factions, Calendars, Naming

## What it is

Create and manage the story's world — locations, systems (magic, politics, technology, religion, economy, military, social, education), factions, artifacts, calendars, routes and travel, naming languages, and glossary terms. Every element cross-references characters and other elements. The world exists to pressure the characters and embody the counter-premise; a setting detail nothing can use is decoration.

## Element types and the questions each answers

| Type | Core questions |

## Economy, logistics, costs

Readers forgive dragons but not armies that never eat. Record prices and wages anchored to wages, not modern money. For any city/army/siege the plot depends on, work out supply lines — a cut supply line sets a deadline in days. **Every power needs a price the story enforces: cost, limit, recovery, side effects, scarcity.** A travel-speeds table (on foot, horse, wagon, barge, ship, train, car) sets plausible route hours.

## Maps and routes

A story map is a graph before a picture: which places connect, how long, by what means. Record routes on the location (destination, hours, mode); two-way unless declared otherwise. The continuity scan errors when a character appears in two dated scenes at routable locations and the story time between them is shorter than the fastest route.

## Calendars, seasons, moons

A secondary world's calendar needs one written record or dates drift. Record months/lengths, weekdays, eras, hours-per-day; date scenes in the calendar; record seasons, moons, and holidays alongside. The phase of a moon on day N is `(N - known full day) mod period`; two moons align every LCM of their periods (a good rare event).

## Naming

Names carry a culture's sound. A phonology sketch per culture (sounds used/never used, syllable shape, stress, common endings, spelling conventions) keeps names related and cultures distinct — change at least two of sound set, syllable shape, and endings to separate cultures. Check every candidate for exact clashes and look-alikes before committing.
