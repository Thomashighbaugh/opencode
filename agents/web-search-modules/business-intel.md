# Business Intelligence Module

> Company research, market data, funding, competitive intel

**Trigger scenarios**: Company due diligence, funding rounds, market sizing, competitor analysis, pricing research

## Search sources
- **Crunchbase** (crunchbase.com) - funding, investors, company data
- **PitchBook** style info, **Tracxn** (tracxn.com)
- **CB Insights** (cbinsights.com)
- **TechCrunch**, **The Information**, **VentureBeat**
- **SEC filings** (sec.gov/EDGAR) for public companies
- **Companies House** (UK), equivalent registries
- **LinkedIn** (for org structure/people - be mindful of access)
- **G2/Capterra/TrustRadius** - product reviews/pricing
- **SimilarWeb** data in articles
- **BuiltWith** (builtwith.com) - tech stack detection

## Query strategy
- Search "[company] funding", "[company] revenue", "[company] competitors"
- Look for recent funding announcements
- Find pricing pages and pricing discussions
- Look for G2 reviews with detailed feedback
- Check job postings for signals (growth/tech stack)
- Find teardown articles

## Information extraction
- Founded, HQ, size, funding history
- Products/pricing, target market
- Competitors, differentiators
- Recent news, leadership
- Customer sentiment
