# Geopolitical Research — Axes, Probes, and Section Template

Use this file for `type == geopolitical` research: country/region dynamics, trade, policy, conflict, sanctions, elections. Load after the user confirms scope.

Probe the user: "What region or country? What aspect — trade, security, domestic politics, international relations? What time horizon?"

## Step 2 axes — Core geopolitical landscape

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Political structure** | Government type, ruling party/coalition, key leaders, power dynamics, recent elections or transitions |
| 2 | **Economic profile** | GDP, major industries, trade partners, currency, debt, economic strengths and vulnerabilities |
| 3 | **International relations** | Key alliances, adversaries, trade agreements, diplomatic tensions, regional influence |
| 4 | **Domestic dynamics** | Major political issues, social movements, public opinion trends, upcoming elections |

## Step 3 axes — Policy and trade

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Trade policy** | Tariffs, trade agreements, export/import composition, trade disputes, supply chain dependencies |
| 2 | **Sanctions and restrictions** | Current sanctions (imposed or received), export controls, financial restrictions, impact assessment |
| 3 | **Regulatory environment** | Business regulations, foreign investment rules, IP protection, compliance requirements for foreign operators |

## Step 4 axes — Conflict and risk

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Security landscape** | Military capabilities, ongoing conflicts, territorial disputes, cyber threats |
| 2 | **Political risk** | Stability indicators, corruption index, rule of law, expropriation risk, contract enforceability |
| 3 | **Scenario analysis** | Best/worst/likely scenarios for the next 1–5 years; trigger events that could shift the trajectory |
