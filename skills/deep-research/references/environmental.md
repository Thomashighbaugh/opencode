# Environmental Research — Axes, Probes, and Section Template

Use this file for `type == environmental` research: climate, sustainability, natural resources, environmental policy, ecological impact. Load after the user confirms scope.

Probe the user: "What environmental topic? Climate, biodiversity, pollution, resource management, energy transition? What scale — local, regional, global?"

## Step 2 axes — Environmental landscape

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Current state** | What is the current state of the environment in this domain? Key indicators, trends, thresholds |
| 2 | **Drivers and pressures** | What human activities drive environmental change in this area? Scale, trend, geographic distribution |
| 3 | **Impacts** | What are the observed and projected impacts? On ecosystems, human health, economy, infrastructure |
| 4 | **Vulnerable systems** | Which ecosystems, species, or communities are most vulnerable? Tipping points, irreversibility |

## Step 3 axes — Policy and response

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Policy landscape** | What policies, treaties, and regulations address this issue? International, national, local — effectiveness assessment |
| 2 | **Technology solutions** | What technologies exist or are emerging to address this? Readiness level, cost, scalability, side effects |
| 3 | **Market mechanisms** | Carbon pricing, cap-and-trade, subsidies, green bonds — what economic instruments are in play? |

## Step 4 axes — Scenarios and action

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Scenario analysis** | Best/worst/likely scenarios for the next 10–50 years. What would change the trajectory? |
| 2 | **Adaptation strategies** | What adaptation measures are needed or underway? Infrastructure, ecosystem restoration, community resilience |
| 3 | **Stakeholder action** | What are governments, corporations, NGOs, and communities doing? Who leads, who lags, who blocks? |
