# Operational Research — Axes, Probes, and Section Template

Use this file for `type == operational` research: processes, supply chains, logistics, organizational design, operational efficiency. Load after the user confirms scope.

Probe the user: "What operational domain? Supply chain, manufacturing, logistics, org design, process optimization? What industry or company size?"

## Step 2 axes — Operational landscape

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Process mapping** | What are the core processes? How do they connect? Where are the bottlenecks, handoffs, and failure points? |
| 2 | **Supply chain structure** | Suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, logistics providers — who are the key players, concentration risk, geographic exposure |
| 3 | **Technology stack** | What software, automation, and tools are used? ERP, WMS, TMS, MES — adoption rates, integration challenges |
| 4 | **Performance benchmarks** | Industry-standard KPIs: cycle time, fill rate, inventory turns, OEE, on-time delivery — how do leaders perform? |

## Step 3 axes — Efficiency and optimization

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Best practices** | What approaches are considered best practice? Lean, Six Sigma, TOC, agile — adoption rates and evidence of impact |
| 2 | **Technology trends** | Automation, AI/ML, IoT, digital twins — what's being adopted, what's hype, what's proven |
| 3 | **Cost drivers** | Where do costs concentrate? Labor, materials, logistics, overhead — benchmarking data |

## Step 4 axes — Risk and resilience

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Supply chain risk** | Single points of failure, geographic concentration, supplier financial health, geopolitical exposure |
| 2 | **Resilience strategies** | Nearshoring, dual sourcing, inventory buffers, scenario planning — what are leaders doing? |
| 3 | **Organizational design** | Team structures, decision rights, incentive alignment, talent gaps — how does org design affect operational performance? |
