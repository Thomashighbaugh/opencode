# Cultural Research — Axes, Probes, and Section Template

Use this file for `type == cultural` research: social movements, media landscape, public opinion, cultural norms, consumer behavior. Load after the user confirms scope.

Probe the user: "What cultural phenomenon? Are you looking at a movement, a shift in norms, media coverage, or public opinion? What population or geography?"

## Step 2 axes — Cultural landscape

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Movement overview** | What is the movement/phenomenon? Origin, key events, current state, stated goals vs actual impact |
| 2 | **Key figures and organizations** | Who are the leaders, influencers, organizations? What platforms do they control? |
| 3 | **Media coverage** | How is this covered in mainstream media? Framing, tone, volume of coverage, key narratives |
| 4 | **Public opinion** | What do surveys and polls show? Demographic breakdowns, trend over time, regional variation |

## Step 3 axes — Social dynamics

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Online discourse** | Social media sentiment, hashtag analysis, community formation, echo chambers, radicalization vectors |
| 2 | **Counter-movements** | Opposition groups, their arguments, their support base, their media presence |
| 3 | **Institutional response** | How have governments, corporations, universities, and other institutions responded? Policy changes, statements, accommodations |

## Step 4 axes — Impact and trajectory

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Behavioral change** | Measurable changes in behavior, consumption, voting, or social practice attributable to this movement |
| 2 | **Cultural artifacts** | Books, films, music, art, memes, language changes — the cultural products this movement has produced or influenced |
| 3 | **Trajectory and scenarios** | Is this growing, peaking, or declining? What would accelerate or reverse it? Historical analogues |
