# Ethical Research — Axes, Probes, and Section Template

Use this file for `type == ethical` research: ethics frameworks, stakeholder impact, governance models, responsible innovation. Load after the user confirms scope.

Probe the user: "What ethical question? Are you evaluating a technology, a policy, a business practice, or a research direction? Who are the affected stakeholders?"

## Step 2 axes — Ethical landscape

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Ethical frameworks** | Which ethical frameworks apply? Consequentialist, deontological, virtue ethics, care ethics — what does each say? |
| 2 | **Stakeholder mapping** | Who is affected? Direct vs indirect stakeholders, vulnerable populations, future generations, non-human stakeholders |
| 3 | **Current debates** | What are the active ethical debates? Who holds which positions? What arguments do they use? |
| 4 | **Precedents** | How have similar ethical questions been resolved in the past? What guidelines, codes, or regulations exist? |

## Step 3 axes — Impact assessment

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Benefit distribution** | Who benefits? How are benefits distributed? Are there winners and losers? |
| 2 | **Harm assessment** | Who could be harmed? What are the worst-case scenarios? Are harms reversible? |
| 3 | **Power dynamics** | Does this concentrate or distribute power? Who gains decision-making authority? Who loses it? |

## Step 4 axes — Governance and mitigation

| Agent | Axis | Key questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Governance models** | What governance structures exist or are proposed? Oversight boards, audit requirements, transparency mechanisms |
| 2 | **Mitigation strategies** | What can be done to reduce harms? Technical safeguards, policy interventions, design choices |
| 3 | **Accountability mechanisms** | Who is responsible when things go wrong? Legal liability, professional standards, enforcement mechanisms |
